Dating Safety Tips

Last updated October 20, 2026

If you're in danger

Call 911. Then report the person in Cupid so we can act.

Stay on Cupid at first

Chat on Cupid until you feel comfortable. Never send money, and be wary of anyone who asks.

Meet in public

Meet somewhere public and busy, get there and back on your own, and tell a friend where you'll be.

Watch your drink

Keep your drink with you and know your limits.

Trust your gut

You can leave a date or unmatch at any time. Report anything that feels off; they won't know it was you.

Get support

RAINN sexual assault hotline: 1-800-656-4673 (online.rainn.org). National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788. 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988.

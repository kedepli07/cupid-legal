Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

The short version

We collect what you put on your profile so Cupid can show it to other people and find matches. We never sell your data and never share it for advertising. You can delete everything at any time.

What we collect

Account: your email, password (stored securely, we can't see it) and mobile number (to verify your account and let you log in with a text code). Profile: first name, birthday (only your age is shown, and your star sign if you choose), school or job, photos, reels, prompts, interests and lifestyle answers. Activity: who you like or pass on, your comments, matches, messages, blocks and reports. Verification: the selfie you take to verify, kept in a private folder only our review team can see. Location: your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) while you use the app, to show you people nearby. Contacts: only if you use Block contacts, and only the contacts you pick, as scrambled (hashed) phone numbers so those people and you don't see each other. We never upload names or your whole contact list. Device: basic technical data needed to run the app, such as app version, error logs and a push notification token if you turn notifications on.

What other people see

Other Cupid users can see your profile: name, age, school or job, photos, reels, prompts, interests, lifestyle answers and your star sign if you chose to show it. They also see roughly how far away you are, in whole miles. They never see your email, phone number, birthday, exact location, swipes or selfie. Your messages are only visible to the person you're chatting with, and to our team when a chat is reported. Photo and reel files are stored at links that anyone with the link can open, so don't post anything you wouldn't want shared.

How we use it

To run Cupid: show profiles, make matches, deliver messages, keep accounts secure, verify profiles, review reports, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with the law. We may use aggregate, de-identified numbers to improve the app.

Who we share it with

Service providers who run Cupid for us, under contracts that limit how they use your data: Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), Twilio (texting verification codes), Expo (delivering push notifications) and Apple (App Store, notifications and payments). We share data with law enforcement only when the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm. If Cupid is sold or merged, your data may move to the new owner under this policy. We do not sell or "share" your personal information for targeted advertising.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, reels, matches, messages and selfie. We may keep reports about you, including copies of reported chats, for up to 2 years to keep the community safe, and anything the law requires us to keep.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile any time, and delete your account from Profile > Delete account. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and other states), you may have the right to know what data we have about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, and opt out of sales, targeted ads and profiling. We don't do those last three. To make a request, email the support email on our App Store page. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Sensitive information

Using a dating app can say something about your love life, and some answers (like drinking or weed) are personal. We use them only to run Cupid and only show them where you chose to put them on your profile.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and our database only lets each person read and change what they're allowed to. No system is perfect, so use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.

Under 18

Cupid is only for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we delete accounts we find that belong to minors. Report a minor from their profile.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app first.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: the support email on our App Store page.

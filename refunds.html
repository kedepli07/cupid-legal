Cupid is free

You can make a profile, match and chat for free. Cupid+ is an optional subscription.

Clear prices

Before you buy Cupid+, the App Store shows the price, how often you're charged and what's included. There are no hidden or extra fees. Until payments launch, Cupid+ is a free preview and you are never charged.

Cancelling

Cancel anytime in your iPhone's Settings > your name > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends. You keep Cupid+ until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the Cupid app or your account doesn't cancel an Apple subscription on its own.

Refunds

Apple bills every purchase, so Apple decides refunds under its policies. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something went wrong on our side, email us too and we'll help.

Contact

Billing questions: the support email on our App Store page.

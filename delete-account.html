Delete Your Account

Last updated October 26, 2026

In the app

Open Cupid, go to Profile, scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm. It happens right away.

Without the app

Email the support email on our App Store page from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your profile, photos, reels, prompts, likes, matches, messages, verification selfie and phone number. Reports other people made about you, with copies of reported chats, may be kept for up to 2 years for safety, plus anything the law requires us to keep.

Just want a break?

Turn on Pause my profile in Profile to hide from new people without deleting anything. To get a copy of your data first, email us.

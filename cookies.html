Cookie Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version

Cupid doesn't use cookies to track you. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs, and these web pages set no cookies, so there is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner.

In the app

The app stores a few things on your phone so it works: your sign-in session and small settings like whether you turned on the Cupid+ preview. These never leave your phone except to keep you signed in to Cupid.

On this website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data like IP addresses to keep its service secure; see GitHub's privacy statement. We don't add analytics or cookies.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies that aren't strictly needed, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first.

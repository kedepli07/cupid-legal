Community Guidelines

Last updated October 20, 2026

Be yourself

Use your own photos and real details. No fake profiles, catfishing or pretending to be someone else.

18+ only

Everyone on Cupid must be 18 or older. No photos of children on their own. Report anyone who seems underage.

Keep it respectful

No harassment, threats, bullying, hate speech or slurs. No unwanted sexual messages. If someone says no or stops replying, leave it.

No nudity or sexual content

No nudity, sexual content or sexually suggestive photos and reels. Swimwear is fine; explicit content is not. Never share someone else's intimate images.

No violence or illegal stuff

No violence, gore, weapons for sale, drugs for sale, or encouraging self-harm.

No scams or selling

No asking for money, crypto, gift cards or investments. No ads, promotions or sex work.

Only reels you can share

Upload reels you made or have the right to share. We remove reels when the owner asks.

What happens if you break these

We remove content, and may suspend or ban accounts, at our discretion. Serious cases, like threats or anything involving minors, are banned on sight and reported to the authorities.
